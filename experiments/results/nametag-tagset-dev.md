three tagsets (conll, its default and the one every earlier run used; uner; onto), beside the
rules. Persons are PER (conll, uner) or PERSON (onto); no tagset has an address type. The
model was trained on CNEC 2.0 and UNER Slovak-SNK train, so those two corpora favour it;
REDACT and OpenPII are the fairer comparisons. Same splits, seed and resamples as nametag-dev.
